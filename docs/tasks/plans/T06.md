# T06 App shell and design system: implementation plan

Branch: `task/T06-app-shell` (worktree `/home/azael/Documents/GitHub/gza-T06`), based on
`main` after T01. Facts below were checked on **2026-09-28**: library versions on Google

## 1. Goal

Give Gza its own look and a real app skeleton. `:core:designsystem` gets a brick-and-limestone
colour identity (light and dark, no dynamic colour), three bundled typefaces that all render
Georgian or the digits they are used for (display, body, mono for times), and the
components every later screen is built from: route badge per transport kind, the four
departure status chips (with the terminus **waiting** chip visually unmistakable, PLAN.md
2.0), a leave-by countdown, a departure row, a bottom sheet scaffold, a banner, a top app
bar and a bottom navigation bar. `:app` gets type-safe Navigation Compose with a bottom
bar over five destinations (Now, Search, Map, Plan, Settings), each a new `:feature:*`
module showing a clearly labelled sample-data placeholder built from the real components,
so the user's phone shows the actual look of the app. All strings exist in en and ka.
Everything is proven by Roborazzi screenshots (every component and every destination,
light and dark), Compose semantics tests for content descriptions and 48dp targets, a
bottom-bar navigation UI test, and a Maestro flow that taps through all five tabs.

### 3.2 Colour identity

Decision: **brick and limestone**, not green and not Material purple. Reasons:
- The current green primary (`#1B6B50`) sits right next to TTC's bus colour (`00B38B`)
  and would make every brand surface read as "bus". Brick is far from every TTC transport
  colour (teal, blue, red, green, orange) so badges always stand out.
- Brick is Tbilisi: the Abanotubani baths and old-town brick; the neutrals are warm
  limestone. It is a distinct identity, not a stock Material seed.

**Transit colours** (`theme/TransitColors.kt`), TTC's own colours so badges match the
signs and buses people see (values from the live `/v3/routes`, see TTC_API.md):
`Bus = 0xFF00B38B`, `Minibus = 0xFF0033B4`, `MetroLine1 = 0xFFFF505B`,

Badge text colour is computed, never hand-picked: `badgeContentColor(bg)` returns ink
`0xFF0B0F0E` or white, whichever has the higher WCAG contrast. Result: bus, metro and
cable car get ink (7.2, 6.0, 6.2, 7.6), minibus gets white (9.9). White on bus teal would
be 2.7, which is why this is computed.

**Dynamic colour: off.** Kept from T01 and now justified by design: route badges and
status chips carry meaning through colour, and a wallpaper-derived primary could land on
amber, green or red and collide with them; the brand identity is the point of T06; and
screenshots must be deterministic. An opt-in toggle can come after MVP; not in T06.

Dark theme follows the system (`isSystemInDarkTheme()`). No in-app theme switch in T06.

### 3.3 Typography

Three bundled faces, all SIL OFL 1.1 without a Reserved Font Name (checked in each
`OFL.txt`), so subsetting and instancing are allowed. Glyph coverage was checked with
fontTools on the exact files:

| Role | Face and instance | Georgian Mkhedruli U+10D0-10FF | Latin + Latin-1 | Cyrillic | Digits | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | **Noto Sans Georgian**, width axis at 75 (condensed), weights 600 and 800 | 48/48 (plus Mtavruli 46/46) | 95/95, 84/96 | none | tabular by default (all digits one advance) | Condensed bold reads like transit signage, fits long Georgian headlines on a phone, and route numbers in badges stay one width. |
| Body | **FiraGO** 1.001 (bBox Type), Regular 400, Medium 500, SemiBold 600 | 48/48 | 95/95, 96/96 | 66/66 | proportional, has `tnum` | Designed as a Georgian + Latin + Cyrillic superfamily, so en, ka and later ru look like one voice. Humanist and very legible at small sizes. |
| Mono (times only) | **Martian Mono**, width 75 (condensed), weights 500 and 700 | none (never gets Georgian) | 95/95 | not needed | monospaced | Departure-board feel, square colon, compact at wdth 75 so `17:13` fits rows; distinct from both other faces. |

Rejected alternatives: JetBrains Mono and IBM Plex Mono (dotted zero is noisy at row
size), Roboto Mono and Geist Mono (fine but generic), Google Sans (10 MB family),
Noto Sans Georgian as body too (no Cyrillic, misses `±` and arrows).

**Bundled, not downloadable.** Decision: ship the fonts in
`core/designsystem/src/main/res/font/`. Reasons: FiraGO is not on Google Fonts at all, and
the condensed static instances we want are not served by the provider; downloadable fonts
need Play services and network on first run and draw a fallback face until loaded; and
Robolectric cannot fetch them, so screenshots would not show the real type.

Size budget after instancing and subsetting (measured): body 3 x ~146 KB, display
2 x ~50 KB, mono 2 x ~22 KB, total **~585 KB**. Files (resource names must be
lowercase):

```

  gza_display_extrabold.ttf  Noto Sans Georgian wght=800 wdth=75
  gza_body_regular.ttf       FiraGO Regular
  gza_body_medium.ttf        FiraGO Medium
  gza_body_semibold.ttf      FiraGO SemiBold
  gza_mono_medium.ttf        Martian Mono wght=500 wdth=75
```

They are produced by a committed, reproducible script `tools/build-fonts.sh` (3.5) from
pinned upstream files. Licences are shipped in the APK as assets:
`core/designsystem/src/main/assets/licenses/{OFL-FiraGO.txt,OFL-NotoSansGeorgian.txt,OFL-MartianMono.txt,Apache-2.0-MaterialSymbols.txt}`
(T16's About screen can list them).

`GzaTypography` = M3 default `Typography()` sizes with families swapped:
`display*`, `headline*`, `titleLarge` use `GzaDisplayFamily` at `FontWeight.SemiBold`
(displayLarge/Medium at `ExtraBold`); `titleMedium`, `titleSmall`, `body*`, `label*` use
`GzaBodyFamily` (titles and labels `Medium`, body `Normal`). Keep M3 default sizes and line
heights (Georgian has tall ascenders; the defaults leave room, screenshots confirm).

Extended styles (`theme/GzaTypography.kt`, `@Immutable data class GzaExtendedTypography`,
`LocalGzaTypography`), all mono unless stated:

| Style | Family, weight | Size / line height | Used by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `timeHero` | mono Bold | 64sp / 64sp, letterSpacing -1sp | countdown hero number |
| `timeLarge` | mono Bold | 24sp / 28sp | departure time in rows |
| `timeMedium` | mono Medium | 16sp / 20sp | leave-by, chip times |
| `timeSmall` | mono Medium | 13sp / 16sp | plan legs |
| `routeNumber` | display ExtraBold | 16sp / 20sp | medium route badge |
| `routeNumberLarge` | display ExtraBold | 22sp / 26sp | large badge |
| `routeNumberSmall` | display ExtraBold | 13sp / 16sp | small badge |

Everything is in `sp`, so it follows the system font size (dynamic type). The mono face
is only ever given strings of digits, `:`, `+`, `-`, `±`, `h` and spaces: a KDoc on
`GzaMonoFamily` says so. If Georgian ever reached it, Android's per-glyph fallback would
still draw it in the system face (no tofu), just not in Martian.

Mono spans inside translated sentences (for example `გადის 17:13-ზე`) use a helper
`withMonoSpan(full: String, value: String, style: SpanStyle): AnnotatedString` in
`component/TextSpans.kt`: finds the first occurrence of `value` in `full` and styles that
range; if not found, returns plain text. The string resource keeps the time as a `%1$s`
argument so each language places it naturally.

`theme/Theme.kt`:
```kotlin
@Composable
fun GzaTheme(darkTheme: Boolean = isSystemInDarkTheme(), content: @Composable () -> Unit) {
    val colors = if (darkTheme) DarkGzaColors else LightGzaColors
    CompositionLocalProvider(LocalGzaColors provides colors, LocalGzaTypography provides GzaExtendedTypography()) {
        MaterialTheme(
            colorScheme = if (darkTheme) DarkColors else LightColors,
            typography = GzaTypography,
            shapes = GzaShapes,
            content = content,
        )
    }
}

object GzaTheme {
    val colors: GzaColors @Composable @ReadOnlyComposable get() = LocalGzaColors.current
    val typography: GzaExtendedTypography @Composable @ReadOnlyComposable get() = LocalGzaTypography.current
}
```
(A function and an object with the same name is the `MaterialTheme` pattern and compiles.)
`LightGzaColors`/`DarkGzaColors` need the scheme's error and outline colours: build them
from `LightColors`/`DarkColors` fields, not duplicated literals.

`theme/Shape.kt`: `GzaShapes = Shapes(extraSmall = 4.dp, small = 8.dp, medium = 16.dp, large = 24.dp, extraLarge = 32.dp)` (rounded corners), `RouteBadgeShape = RoundedCornerShape(6.dp)`.

### 3.4 Components (`:core:designsystem`, package `ge.hackerman.gza.core.designsystem.component`)

Every component is stateless, takes `modifier: Modifier = Modifier` as its first optional
parameter, reads colours from `MaterialTheme`/`GzaTheme`, and has a `@PreviewLightDark`
preview wrapped in `GzaTheme` (plus an extra `@Preview(locale = "ka")` where text length
matters). Designsystem does **not** depend on `:core:model`: it defines small UI enums and
feature code maps domain types onto them (T07). Reason: keeps the design system reusable
and free of domain churn while T03/T05 shape the model.

the state survives tab switches through `saveState`/`restoreState`.

3. **Espresso on SDK 36+.** The screenshot plugin already pins espresso-core 3.7.0 for
   test classpaths; `:core:testing` must also expose it (api) or modules that only use
   `:core:testing` hit `InputManager.getInstance()` crashes.
4. **Lint `warningsAsErrors` surprises.** Expect `PluralsCandidate`, `MissingQuantity`
   (ka needs `one` and `other`), `UnusedResources` (fonts, icons or `sample_*` committed
   before use, see steps 1, 5, 10), `VectorPath` (suppressed per path),
   `AutoboxingStateCreation` (use `mutableIntStateOf`), Compose API lint
   (`ModifierParameter`: modifier is the first optional parameter;
   `ComposableLambdaParameterNaming/Position`: trailing composable lambda named `content`).
   Fix at the source; suppress only with `tools:ignore`/`@Suppress` plus a reason.
5. **detekt `LongMethod` (60 lines) and `TooManyFunctions` (11 per file)** on screen
   files: split sections into private composables and files.
6. **`clearAndSetSemantics` on the clickable row.** The row sets `role` and `onClick`
   again inside `clearAndSetSemantics`, so the click action survives whatever the
   modifier order does. `DepartureRowTest` asserts it; if it fails, print the tree with
   `onRoot().printToLog("tree")` and fix the modifier order, not the test.
7. **"Content description" vs visible labels.** Nav items, segmented buttons and text
   fields are named by their visible label (text semantics); adding the same text as a
   content description would make TalkBack read it twice. The helper accepts either.
   If the reviewer insists on a literal content description, set it with
   `clearAndSetSemantics` on the item and re-add `role`, `selected` and `onClick`, but
   flag the TalkBack double-read trade-off to the manager first.
8. **M3 bottom sheet semantics in Robolectric.** The expand/collapse actions live on a
   wrapper around the drag handle; find the node with `hasKey(SemanticsActions.Expand)`
   rather than by tag. If Robolectric does not settle the sheet animation, call
   `composeRule.mainClock.advanceTimeBy(1_000)` before asserting.
9. **No animations in T06.** The live dot is static and the countdown does not tick
   (static sample). An infinite pulse would make `waitForIdle` in screenshot tests
   unreliable; T07 can add it behind `LocalInspectionMode` or a parameter.
10. **Georgian copy quality.** The ka strings were written for this plan; ask the user to
    glance at the ka goldens / phone for wording (especially `status_waiting`,
    `countdown_*`, `plan_*`). Changing a string later only needs goldens re-recorded.
11. **APK size.** Fonts add ~585 KB. Acceptable; T16's R8/resource shrinking does not
    touch fonts. If size matters later, drop FiraGO Medium (use SemiBold for labels).
12. **Golden churn in the repo.** About 40 goldens. Components are captured at their own
    bounds to keep them small; screens are full size (a few hundred KB each). Fine for
    now; revisit Git LFS only if the repo grows past ~50 MB.
13. **Mono face is digits only.** Anyone passing words to `timeHero`/`time*` styles gets
    system-font fallback for Georgian. The KDoc on `GzaMonoFamily` says so; reviewers
    should reject such uses.
14. **Pinned upstream font URLs.** GitHub raw URLs at commit SHAs are stable; if one ever
    disappears, the committed TTFs remain the source of truth and the script only needs a
    new URL with the same sha256.

## 7. Implementation notes (what changed from this plan)

- **Touch target check uses the layout size.** `touchBoundsInRoot` is widened by Compose to
  the 48dp minimum for every pointer-input node, so a check on it can never fail;
  `boundsInRoot` is clipped by scrolling. `assertInteractiveNodesAccessible` checks
  `SemanticsNode.size`, widened only by a Material `minimumInteractiveComponentSize()` on the
  same layout (so a 40dp `IconButton` in its 48dp target passes, a padded 32dp box fails).
  It also fails a content description that repeats the node's own text. It caught a real bug: M3 `SegmentedButton`s are 40dp tall, so Plan
  gives them `heightIn(min = 48.dp)`.
- **Bottom sheet semantics (risk 8 was real).** Material 1.4 exposes no Expand/Collapse
  actions, and its handle slot stacks a tooltip box on a click box, so TalkBack stopped on the
  handle twice. `GzaBottomSheetScaffold` leaves the slot empty and puts its own handle at the
  top of the sheet content: one node that toggles on click and offers Expand or Collapse.
  A `dragHandleModifier` parameter lets Map tag it.
- **Departure row layout.** Destination and time share the top line; leave-by, detail and
  the chip sit under the destination at full width. The end-column layout squeezed long
  Georgian chips into five lines and broke words at 1.5x font size. The badge sits in a
  60dp slot so rows line up whatever the badge width.
- **Navigation labels shrink to fit** (`TextAutoSize`, 12sp down to 11sp, 4dp side padding)
  and ellipsize below that, one line: "პარამეტრები" does not fit a fifth of a phone even at
  11sp, so it shows as "პარამეტ..." while TalkBack still reads the whole label. Two lines
  would break the single word in the middle. A shorter Georgian label is a product call.
- **Screenshot helper draws on a `Surface`**, so components that inherit
  `LocalContentColor` (the countdown) get `onBackground`, not black.
- **Fonts load in Robolectric** (risk 1 did not happen): the goldens show the bundled faces.
- **detekt `MatchingDeclarationName`**: size and tone enums live in their own files
  (`RouteBadgeSize.kt`, `BannerTone.kt`, `CountdownDisplay.kt`); the `XNavigation.kt`
  files suppress it per file, since one route key plus graph functions is the convention.
- **Strings**: `map_title` is not added (the Map screen has no top bar, so it would be
  unused). Sample strings were added in the step that first uses them (lint
  `UnusedResources`), and features were wired into `:app` one by one for the same reason.
- **Extra goldens**: `plan_screen_ka`, and the banner, top app bar and sheet goldens come
  from one `ChromeScreenshotTest`. The typography and departure row goldens use a taller
  window so every style fits.
- **Map sheet** peeks at 232dp so the waiting 326 row shows without dragging; the sample
  banner moved below the rows.
- **Top app bar is a plain row, not Material's TopAppBar.** Material's small bar is a fixed
  64dp, so a subtitle that wraps at 1.5x font size was clipped. `GzaTopAppBar` keeps the
  64dp minimum and the 16dp title inset, applies the top and side safe-drawing insets and
  grows with its text.
